Welcome to ExamPaper Studio

Create clean, print-ready A4 school exam papers offline in minutes:

  • 1. Pick a Header or Template: Use Titles or Templates on the left to add an exam header or full paper.
  • 2. Click Anywhere to Type: Click any text or header field directly on the page to edit it. Drag elements to move, or drag corners to resize.
  • 3. Type in Nepali (Preeti): Select any text box and click नेपाली (Preeti) in the floating bar to open the Romanized & Unicode helper.
  • 4. Add Answer Lines & Marks: Use Lines to insert solid, dotted, or dashed answer lines, or quick-insert right-aligned marks like [5].
  • 5. Print or Save PDF: Click Print or Export PDF in the top bar for exact A4 pages with zero browser headers.
